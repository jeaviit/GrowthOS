## Services

### 1. Inbound AI Receptionist
**Who it's for:** Med spas, dental clinics, hair/nail/beauty salons, home service businesses (HVAC, landscaping, roofers)

**The problem:** Local service businesses rely on the phone but their staff only works 9–6. Every missed call outside those hours is a lost lead — and the business owner often has no idea what it's costing them. One missed call = $1,500–$5,000 depending on the niche.

**The solution:** A 24/7 AI voice agent that:
- Answers every inbound call, day or night
- Books appointments directly into the calendar
- Answers FAQs about the business
- Transfers calls when needed
- Filters spam

**Why these niches:** High inbound call volume, high-ticket customers, owners are reachable via cold call (Google Maps).

---

### 2. Lead Reactivation System
**Who it's for:** Any business with a CRM full of old leads — med spas, home services, real estate, agencies, high-ticket service businesses.

**The problem:** Most businesses that have run ads have a list of leads who opted in, showed interest, and then went cold. The business already paid to acquire those leads. They're sitting unused in a spreadsheet or CRM.

**The solution:** An AI follow-up system that goes through the old lead list, reaches out to each contact with a personalised message or promotion, reengages them, and books appointments automatically.


**Pricing option:** Performance-based — charge per booked appointment (not per closed deal, since close rate is outside our control).

---

### 3. Speed to Lead System
**Who it's for:** Any business running paid ads — real estate, dental clinics, med spas, home services, agencies.

**The problem:** Most businesses follow up with new leads hours or even a day later. Data shows leads contacted within 5 minutes are ~100x more likely to convert vs. 1 hour later. Most businesses lose deals not because their ads are bad, but because their follow-up is slow.

**The solution:** The moment a lead fills out a form, clicks an ad, or opts in anywhere, an AI voice agent calls them back automatically within 5 minutes. No human needed. The agent introduces itself, qualifies the lead, answers basic questions, and books them into the calendar.

## Pricing Strategy

**Model:** Value-based pricing — charge based on value created, not time.
**Structure:** Setup fee + monthly retainer.

**How to anchor price:** Ask "What is one missed call worth to this business?" Then charge a fraction of the monthly value created.

Example for a med spa averaging $400/treatment:
- 5 missed calls/week = $2,000/week = $8,000/month in lost revenue
- Charge 20–25% of that = $1,600–$2,000 setup fee
- System pays for itself in under a month

**Setup fees:** $2,000–$15,000 depending on complexity and business size
**Monthly retainer:** 20% of setup fee (~$400–$3,000/month)
Retainer covers: maintenance, monitoring, optimisation, one strategy call/month, one small automation upgrade/month, priority support.

**Lead Reactivation pricing option:** Per booked appointment (performance-based). Removes risk for client, easier to close.

**Payment terms:** 50% upfront / 50% on completion. Or 100% upfront. No payment = no work started, no exceptions.

## Sales Process

Two-step process: Discovery Call → Proposal Call (only if qualified)

Full flow: Outreach → Booked call → (if no-show, follow up) → Discovery call → Proposal call → Close

---

### Discovery Call
**Purpose:** Diagnose the business, identify the real problem, qualify the client, and build the ROI case to justify the price.

**Key questions to ask (example for dental/inbound):**
- What got you interested in voice agents?
- What problem are you trying to solve?
- How many calls do you get per day?
- How many are missed?
- What happens after hours?
- How many appointments come through calls?
- What is one new patient worth?
- How many hours does your receptionist work? What's their salary?

**For lead reactivation:** Focus on CRM size, lead age, current follow-up process, close rates.
**For speed to lead:** Focus on ad spend, current follow-up time, lead-to-booking conversion rate.

**Outcome:** Decide if qualified → identify the pain → build the ROI case → move to proposal call or disqualify.

---

### Proposal Call
**Purpose:** Present the solution and move to a decision.

**Simple structure:**
1. "You have this problem."
2. "It is costing you this much."
3. "Here is the solution."
4. "It will save/make you this much."
5. "Here is the price, timeline, and how it works."

**Outcome is always one of three:** Yes / No / Follow-up

**If yes — close on the call:**
- Send contract immediately
- Send payment link immediately
- Get payment while still on the call
- A verbal yes is not a closed deal. Payment = client.

## Delivery Process — 5-Step Framework

### Step 1: Payment & Proposal
No payment, no work. 50% upfront / 50% on completion (or 100% upfront).

Define "done" upfront in the proposal per service:
- **Inbound Receptionist:** Agent built, knowledge base loaded, call forwarding configured, tested across question types and edge cases, escalation path working.
- **Lead Reactivation:** Agent built, tested on 10+ leads, auto-dialer deployed, Google Sheet live, first campaign running.
- **Speed to Lead:** Agent built, webhook connected to lead source, sub-5-minute callback verified, end-to-end tested with live form submissions.

Anything outside scope = new invoice. No scope creep.

---

### Step 2: Onboarding Call
Cover three things: timeline, communication, and access.

**Timeline (30-day delivery):**
| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| Week 1 | Account setup, credential collection, lead list cleaning |
| Week 2 | Agent build, prompt customisation, backend deployment |
| Week 3 | Pilot campaign, monitoring, testing, fixing issues |
| Week 4 | Final handoff, documentation, retainer kickoff |

**Communication:** WhatsApp only. Response within 24 hours (not weekends). No scattered channels.

**Infrastructure model (client-owned):** Client creates their own Retell, Modal, and API accounts. We get added as collaborator. They pay API costs. We build and manage everything, charge the retainer.

Collect all credentials before ending the onboarding call.

---

### Step 3: Weekly Progress Updates
Every Monday morning, no exceptions. Even if progress was slow.

Template (WhatsApp):
"Hey [name] — week [X] update: Completed: [specific thing]. This week: [what's next]. On track for [delivery date]. I'll have something to show you by [specific date]. Any questions, I'm here."

Short. Specific. Confident. Never vague.

---

### Step 4: Stress Testing
Never hand over a system that hasn't been tested to failure. Test:
- Happy path (full intended flow)
- Edge cases: hostile leads, wrong name, "are you an AI?", voicemail, transfer fails, unexpected timeline answers, fast/slow speech, strong accents, API timeout, missing sheet fields
- Consistency: qualifying logic and outcome tagging must be consistent across different conversation paths

Only deliver once the system fails gracefully — even when something goes wrong, it doesn't embarrass the client or lose a lead permanently.

---

### Step 5: Documentation & Delivery

Deliver a full resource package:
- **Loom walkthrough:** Shows the client how to check Retell costs, API usage, database/Google Sheets, and everything they need to manage their side.
- **Written SOP:** Same as the Loom but in written format.

Delivery message template:
"Hey [name] — your [system name] is live and fully tested. Here's everything: Loom walkthrough: [link] | Written SOP: [link]. The system is running. You'll start seeing results populate in the sheet within the first campaign run. Sending the final invoice now — [payment link]. P.S. While building this I noticed [specific observation]. I'd love to do a free audit call to map that out. No obligation, just want to make sure you're getting maximum value."

The P.S. is the upsell opener.
